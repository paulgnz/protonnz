Tax season on XPR Network should not be this hard. The block explorer's built-in export caps at
**10,000 transactions** and silently drops the rest, and mainstream crypto-tax tools don't
understand XPR-native activity — DEX fills, launchpad claims, lending, referral income — and often
price your ecosystem tokens as unrelated coins that happen to share a ticker.

So we built the tool we wished existed: **[tax.protonnz.com](https://tax.protonnz.com)**.

It rebuilds an account's **entire** history directly from the blockchain, classifies every event,
reconciles the totals against your actual on-chain balances, and exports a file you can import
straight into your tax software.

## What it does

- **Your complete history — no 10,000-row limit.** Every transaction since day one, for the busy
  multi-year accounts a quick explorer export can't handle.
- **Every event classified.** Trades, DEX fills, staking rewards, lending income, launchpad claims,
  LP and referral fees — sorted for you, not left as raw transfers.
- **Tokens mapped correctly.** XPR Network tokens, including SimpleDEX ones, are matched to the
  right asset instead of being confused with similarly-named listed coins. No manual relabeling.
- **Reconciled to the chain.** We cross-check several archive nodes, repair any gaps, and prove the
  result against your wallet, staked, DEX and launchpad balances — so nothing is missed.
- **Import-ready.** Exports work with **CoinTracking**, **Koinly** and **Summ**, in your choice of
  currency (USD, NZD, AUD, CAD, GBP, EUR). A reconciliation report is included.
- **Balance time machine.** Reconstruct exactly what any account held at your tax year-end
  (New Zealand, Australia, Canada, UK, US, EU or any date), with USD and local values.

## Why it's more complete, not less

Public history nodes can quietly lose records — dropped documents, collapsed duplicates, fork
ghosts. A single source isn't guaranteed complete. XPR Tax reads from a full-history
[Hyperion archive node](https://hyperion-xpr-mainnet.protonnz.com), cross-checks it against other
nodes, repairs gaps, and then **proves** the result by reconciling every token's ledger total to
its live on-chain balance. If something didn't add up, it would tell you rather than hand you a
wrong number.
