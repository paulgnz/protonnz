Every voter on XPR Network has the same problem: **how do you tell a block producer that ships
from one that just parks a domain?** The producer table gives you a URL. Most producers publish
a working bp.json behind it. So does that make them equal?

No — and here's the part that took us a while to get right.

## Infrastructure is the floor, not the ceiling

Every BP has to run nodes. Every BP has to publish a valid bp.json. Every BP has to keep their
seeds up so other BPs can peer. That's not something to be proud of; it's the **minimum
requirement to be a BP at all**. If a producer can't do that, they shouldn't be in the schedule.

Making a status page, a Code of Conduct, or a snapshot service is nice, and we ship all three
ourselves at ProtonNZ — but they're table stakes for infra. Voting on which BP has the shiniest
status page is like voting on which restaurant has the cleanest kitchen. Yes it matters. No, it
shouldn't be the reason you eat somewhere.

This isn't just our opinion. The official [XPR Network Block Producer Code of
Conduct](https://github.com/XPRNetwork/xpr.start), which every BP is expected to sign, says it
directly in **Section 6.1**:

> BPs shall actively contribute to the growth and development of the XPR Network ecosystem by
> providing educational resources, technical support, and mentorship.

**The real question is: what have they built on XPR Network?**

## The methodology

We took the full list of **active block producers** from the `eosio` producers table
(`is_active == 1`, 37 producers at the time of writing), verified every URL was reachable, then
went through each site and counted the dapps they've shipped **on XPR Network**.

We did not count:

- Nodes, seed servers, Hyperion history, status pages, or any BP infrastructure
- The BP's own marketing site
- Docs of protocol features they didn't build
- Products they ship on other chains (Solana bots, WAX games, EOS wallets, generic multi-chain infra)
- Partner products they resell but didn't build

We did count: **live web apps, wallets, DEXes, DeFi tools, NFT platforms, tax/analytics apps,
bots, browser extensions, mobile apps, games, and SDKs** that run on or exist to serve XPR
Network users.

We did **not** count **thin AI wrappers**: MCP servers that expose XPR tools to LLM agents,
and "chat with XPR" style products that are essentially a system prompt on top of a public
chain. Those are useful, but they're not products a user or developer opens for its own sake
— they're glue for something else.

> **BPs — if we got you wrong, tell us.** This audit is done in good faith from public
> information at a single point in time. If you're a block producer on XPR Network and we've
> missed a dapp you've shipped, mislabelled something you built, or got any of the details
> wrong, please [contact us](/contact) and we'll update the post and the count. The goal is an
> honest picture of the ecosystem, not a scoreboard.

**Treat these numbers as a floor, not a ceiling.** Our method reads what's publicly linked from
each BP's on-chain URL, plus a certificate-transparency sweep for subdomains. That misses
products on entirely separate domains, anything announced only on X or Telegram, and sites
that block automated requests. Several BPs on this list have already been moved *up* after
they pointed us at something we couldn't see — every one of those corrections came from the
BP, not from us. Assume there are more.

## The honest headline

**18 of 36 non-ProtonNZ BPs ship at least one XPR-specific dapp. 4 ship 3 or more. Cafe
(Metallicus) and homebloksbp are tied for second at 5 each; alvosec is next at 4.**

Rounding is doing some work here — Metallicus built the entire network, so ranking their
product output against ours isn't quite fair either direction. But excluding them, **the gap
between ProtonNZ and the next independent builder-BP is 12 vs 5.**

If you filter for "BPs whose product output is roughly proportional to their BP rewards,"
homebloksbp, alvosec, protonind, madblocks, rockeronebp, bloxprod, xprdata and chaininfra are
the ones we'd point voters at first — because they're actively shipping user-facing XPR
software, not just running nodes.

### But a count is a crude proxy

Worth saying plainly, because it's the fairest criticism of this whole exercise: **one
genuinely good product that people use every day contributes more to XPR than ten that nobody
opens.** A BP sitting on a single dapp with real users may well be doing more for the network
than one with five quietly idle ones.

We're counting shipped output because it's the thing we can verify from the outside. So treat
the count as the start of the question, not the answer to it. It tells you who has *built*
something. It doesn't tell you what's *working*.

So we went and looked.

### What the chain actually says

So we went and looked. **Snapshot: 10 September 2026.** For every BP dapp above with an
identifiable contract, we counted the distinct accounts that used it over the six months to
that date — a full census of 10.9 million actions, not a sample.

| Dapp | BP | Distinct users (6 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Metal X swaps | cafe | 5,638 |
| Vibrr | homebloksbp | 4,553 |
| LOAN | cafe | 4,196 |
| Metal X order-book | cafe | 2,366 |
| Chain Runner | protonnz | 1,725 |
| Snipverse | snipverse | 854 |
| SimpleDEX | protonnz | 755 |
| BlastPad | homebloksbp | 715 |
| XPR Casino | protonind | 85 |
| XPR Agents | protonnz | 30 |

Counts are uniform — every distinct account that used the contract, with no judgement about
which users are worth counting.

**Notice how little this correlates with the rankings above.** Vibrr has more distinct users
than SimpleDEX and Chain Runner combined. LOAN alone beats every non-Metallicus dapp except
Vibrr. A BP with two dapps can reach more people than a BP with five, and nothing in a count
of shipped products tells you that.

Some dapps are legitimately invisible in this data and that's fine: portfolio trackers,
whale-watch feeds, tax tools, vote UIs and alert bots *read* the chain rather than write to it.
No contract is not the same as no value.

And the number that makes the case best: **GeneroX has no contract of its own and 33 on-chain
actions in total.** By every metric in this post it barely registers. It has also moved roughly
fifty-nine thousand US dollars of real donations from 68 real people to wildfire and medical-aid
campaigns. Rank purely by count and you rank it near zero. That should tell you exactly how much
weight to put on counting — including the counting we just did.

## A note to fellow BPs

To every producer on this list who hasn't shipped an XPR dapp yet: consider building one.
The Code of Conduct commits every BP to contributing to the growth of the network, and the
most direct way to do that is to ship something users open. It doesn't have to be big — a
small utility, a bot, a niche tool for holders — anything that puts real product into the
ecosystem. The more BPs building, the healthier XPR gets, and we'd be genuinely happy to see
these rankings tighten up.

## The takeaway for voters

**Vote for the BPs building the apps you actually use.**

Running a node is a job. Building a dapp that a real user opens is a different job. Both
matter. But if you want to reward the second, you have to look past the shiny infra pages and
ask: *what did you actually ship for XPR users this year?*

Voting on XPR happens as part of staking — when you stake your XPR, you vote for **up to 4
block producers** at the same time. Even if you already know which BP you like most, you still
have to pick three others. This post exists partly to help with that decision.

If ProtonNZ is one of the four BPs you want backing the network, we'd appreciate one of those
votes. Open [WebAuth](https://webauth.com), tap **XPR → Staking**, add or edit your producer
choices, and include `protonnz` in the four.

*Numbers reflect the state of the active BP list on the date of publication. The field
changes; the check is worth re-running periodically. If we missed a dapp you shipped on XPR
that fits our criteria, let us know via the [contact page](/contact) and we'll update the
count.*
